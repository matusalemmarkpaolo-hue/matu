MINIONS-INSPIRED PORTFOLIO
===========================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract minions_portfolio.zip.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to view the portfolio in your browser.

FILES
- index.html: the portfolio page
- css/style.css: colors, layout, responsive styling
- js/script.js: mobile menu and current year

CUSTOMIZE
- Replace "Your Name" with your name.
- Replace your.email@example.com with your real email address.
- Edit the About, Skills, and Projects sections in index.html.
- Skill progress bars are examples; update them to match your experience.
- Add your own project links where appropriate.

This is a Minions-inspired yellow-and-blue design with a CSS-drawn cartoon-style character. It does not include official Minions images or logos.
